        How to Play

        1. Pick a mystery player. Both players secretly choose one of the 24 characters.
        2. Ask a yes/no question. On your turn, type your own question — "Does your player wear glasses?"
        3. Your opponent answers. They tap YES or NO based on their mystery player.
        4. Knock them down yourself. Tap every portrait the answer rules out. Tapped the wrong one? Tap it again to bring it back. The game never does this for you.
        5. End your turn. Hit Done — End Turn and your opponent goes.
        6. Make your final guess. Think you know? On your turn, tap Make Final Guess. Right = you win. Wrong = you lose. One shot.

        Chat is just for talking trash — it never asks a question, ends a turn or touches the board.

        Lock in your final guess?
